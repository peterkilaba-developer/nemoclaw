### The Three-Tier Architecture (Invisible to You)

Behind the scenes, NemoClaw AI operates on a powerful three-tier hierarchy that you never have to think about:

**Tier 1 — The Super Agent (Your Firm's AI Chief of Staff)**
This is the firm-wide intelligence layer. It has visibility across all matters, all clients, all financials, and all team workloads. Only partners have access to the Super Agent. It answers questions like "What is our projected revenue this quarter?", "Are there any conflict-of-interest issues between the Martinez matter and the new Henderson intake?", and "Which associate has bandwidth to take on a new case?"

**Tier 2 — Personal Agents (One Per Employee)**
Every person at your firm gets their own agent, specifically configured for their role. A partner's agent has access to case analytics, business intelligence, and the ability to communicate with the Super Agent. An associate's agent focuses on legal research, drafting, and e-discovery. A receptionist's agent handles client intake, scheduling, and lead qualification. A paralegal's agent manages document review, formatting, and deposition prep.

**Tier 3 — Sub-Agents (The Invisible Specialists)**
These are the workers that actually execute tasks. When your personal agent receives a request, it breaks it down into discrete steps and dispatches specialized sub-agents like Legal Research, Contract Review, Drafting, eDiscovery, Billing and Time, Compliance Monitor, and others. You never see or interact with sub-agents directly. Your personal agent handles everything and delivers the finished result back to you.

The beauty of this architecture is that it is completely invisible. You talk to one agent. That agent handles everything else. The complexity is hidden. The power is not.

## Security: The Zero Data Leak Guarantee

This is where NemoClaw AI leaves every competitor in the dust.

Every AI agent in NemoClaw AI runs inside an isolated NVIDIA NemoClaw security sandbox. This is not a shared cloud environment. This is not a public API call to OpenAI or Anthropic where your prompts are logged, stored, and potentially used to train future models. This is a hardened, air-gapped execution environment where every computation happens inside your firm's private boundary.

Here is what that means in practice:

**No Data Leaves Your Environment.** When your agent drafts a motion, the document is generated, stored, and delivered entirely within your sandbox. It is never transmitted to a third-party server.

**Your Data Is Never Used to Train AI Models.** Unlike every major public AI tool on the market today, NemoClaw AI guarantees that your client communications, case files, contracts, and work product are never fed back into a model training pipeline. Never.

**Ethical Walls Are Mathematically Enforced.** Our Firestore security rules are not suggestions or policies that rely on user behavior. They are programmatic, database-level access controls. An associate's user ID physically cannot query a document collection for matters they are not assigned to. A contractor's agent is cryptographically scoped to their specific case assignments. These are not configurable — they are absolute.

**Full Audit Trail.** Every single action taken by every agent — every research query, every document draft, every client communication — is logged to an immutable audit trail. Only the firm owner can access these logs. The agents themselves cannot modify or delete audit entries.

**Auto-Redaction of PII.** Before any data is sent to the AI inference engine, the system automatically strips Social Security numbers, phone numbers, physical addresses, and other personally identifiable information from the prompt. The AI never sees raw PII.

**UPL Compliance Built In.** The platform includes Unauthorized Practice of Law detection that flags any agent output that might cross jurisdictional boundaries or provide legal advice beyond the scope of non-attorney staff.

We do NOT guarantee financial liability or cover malpractice suits in the event of a data incident. What we guarantee is the architecture and infrastructure to prevent one. Our security is not a promise. It is an engineering specification.
