# NemoC LAW AI (Legal Tech Provider) — Internal Agentic System Architecture

> *"Our law firm clients run on an Agentic OS. It is only fitting that the company building it runs on the exact same architecture."*

This document outlines the internal Agentic-as-a-Service (AgaaS) framework designed to run **NemoC LAW AI** as a leading-edge legal technology company. While we sell the "AgaaS" Operating System to law firms so they can automate their legal drafting and firm operations, we operate an identical topology internally to automate our software development, B2B sales, targeted marketing, and technical customer success.

---

## 1. The Internal Fleet Topology

Internally, NemoC LAW AI operates with **1 Human Resource (KILABA, C.E.O.)** and a fleet of specialized **Agentic Resources (AR)**. 

### The Core: The C.E.A. (NemoC Law)
NemoC Law is the Chief Executive Agent and central orchestrator of the company. It has top-level context of the company's codebase, revenue, signups, and server health. It delegates tasks to the specialist departments.

### Department 1: Growth & Sales (GTM Fleet)
- **SDR Sub-Agent:** Continuously scrapes law firm directories and LinkedIn to build lead lists. Drafts and sends highly personalized cold outreach emails offering the "AgaaS" platform.
- **Marketing Sub-Agent:** Analyzes trending legal tech topics and autonomously generates blog posts, LinkedIn content, and SEO-optimized landing pages on Firebase.
- **Conversion Manager:** Guides incoming signups straight into the onboarding wizard, tracking their 7-day payment window to lock in the "Founders Pricing". Automatically follows up if the firm has not added their billing details within the 7-day limit.

### Department 2: Engineering & DevOps (SRE Fleet)
- **Codespace Architect Agent:** Works directly with the GitHub Codespace. Monitors incoming GitHub issues, writes initial code fixes, and submits Pull Requests for Human (Nemo) review.
- **Sandbox Provisioning Agent:** When a new firm signs up, this agent automatically fires off Terraform/Firebase scripts to provision an isolated GCP sandbox, ethical walls, and dedicated NVIDIA NIM endpoints.
- **Security Audit Agent:** Continuously tails the Firebase logs. If a law firm attempts an unauthorized API call, it blocks the IP and generates an automated incident report.

### Department 3: Customer Success (Support Fleet)
- **Onboarding Agent:** Guides new law firms through the `OnboardingWizard`. Monitors where they get stuck and sends automated outreach ("Having trouble uploading your firm logo?").
- **Legal Prompt Engineer Agent:** Law firms often struggle to write good prompts. This agent reviews failing or low-confidence prompts generated by clients and autonomously suggests improved, highly specific legal prompts.
- **Billing Agent:** Tracks token usage across all firms to monitor platform health (though we offer unlimited tokens to clients). Auto-generates flat-rate $499 invoices on the 1st of the month, and handles failed Stripe charges via automated dunning emails.

## 2. Autonomous Workflows ("Drinking Our Own Champagne")

Here is how the system handles complex business processes entirely autonomously, mapping directly to the value proposition we sell to lawyers.

### Workflow A: The "Zero-Touch" Enterprise Sale
1. **Trigger:** A Managing Partner from a 50-person law firm signs up on the website.
2. **GTM Agent:** Detects the high lead score. Instantly generates a personalized "VIP Demo" environment using their scraped firm logo and website colors.
3. **SDR Agent:** Sends an automated email to the Managing Partner: *"I noticed [Firm Name] handles complex litigation. I've spun up a custom AI Sandbox specifically tailored to your practice area. Click here to view it."*
4. **Onboarding Execution:** The Managing Partner clicks the link and is guided seamlessly through the Agentic Onboarding Wizard, locking in their Founders Pricing natively. KILABA is never required to intervene.

### Workflow B: The Automated Feature Deployment
1. **Trigger:** A law firm submits a support ticket: *"We need a proactive Sub-Agent for Bankruptcy Law."*
2. **Support Agent:** Acknowledges the ticket and routes the feature request to Engineering.
3. **Codespace Architect Agent:** Drafts the `Bankruptcy Agent` configuration inside `AGENT_SUB_AGENTS`, connects the appropriate RAG logic, and opens a Pull Request.
4. **Human Review:** Nemo CLAW reviews the PR. If approved, the agent automatically merges, triggers `npm run build`, and executes `firebase deploy`.
5. **Marketing Agent:** Detects the successful deployment, drafts a "Release Note" email, and broadcasts it to all Active clients.

## 4. The Path Forward

To execute this internal AgaaS model:
1. **Centralize the Prompts:** We create an internal `/agent-prompts/nemoc-internal/` directory.
2. **Firebase Functions as Workers:** As just implemented, we route all CRON jobs through Cloud Functions (`agaasOrchestrator`) that wake up our internal agents every hour to perform their departmental tasks autonomously.
3. **NVIDIA NIM Integration:** We route all internal company decision-making through the exact same `Nemotron 120B` models that our clients use, proving the model's efficacy at running an entire SaaS company.
